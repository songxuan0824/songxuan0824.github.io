About Me

I am a third-year undergraduate at Tsinghua University, where I joined the Tsien Hsue-shen Elite Class in Mechanics in 2024. I was a visiting student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) from July to August 2026.

My research interests lie in world models, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence.

I’d love to connect, exchange ideas, and explore opportunities to collaborate. Feel free to reach out!

Co-evolution: A "One-to-many" LLM Fine-Tuning Paradigm

Dapeng Jiang, Haichuan Tan, Wenxuan Song, Dianqiao Lei, Shenqi Zong, Yanyan Lan

Co-Evolve turns a single base LLM into a cooperative population of specialized descendants, using marginal coverage and evolution strategies to improve their complementarity.

IPIBench: Evaluating Interactive Proactive Intelligence of MLLMs under Continuous Streams

Jinzhao Li, Yinuo Chen, Wenxuan Song, Yijia Lei, Yichi Zhang, Honglei Yan, Panwang Pan, Miao Liu

Evaluating proactive multimodal assistants over continuous video streams, with evolving user requests and multi-turn interactions. The work also introduces IPI-Agent to improve when and how models respond.
