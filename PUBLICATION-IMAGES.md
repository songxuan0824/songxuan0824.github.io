# Publication illustrations

Retrieved or prepared on 2026-10-24 for publications coauthored by Wenxuan Song.

## Co-evolution

`coevolution-overview.svg` is a new conceptual illustration based on the [NeurIPS 2026 abstract](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/loc/sydney/poster/149378). It shows the one-to-many fine-tuning idea and complementary coverage, and is explicitly labeled as a concept illustration. It is not a figure reproduced from the paper. The three descendant boxes are illustrative, not a claim about a fixed population size.

## IPIBench

`ipibench-overview.png` is the unmodified [official project overview](https://lijinzhao30.github.io/IPIBench/IPIBench.png), 4000 × 2302 pixels. It is byte-identical to [the repository asset](https://github.com/lijinzhao30/IPI/blob/main/assets/IPIBench.png), Git blob SHA `ed9b566466e307ddccc81a01bb9bb6ef161caef7`.

Authors: Jinzhao Li, Yinuo Chen, Wenxuan Song, Yijia Lei, Yichi Zhang, Honglei Yan, Panwang Pan, Miao Liu. The repository is licensed under Apache License 2.0; the full license is included in `IPIBench-LICENSE.txt`.

## PIVOTSBench

`pivotsbench-overview.png` is the unmodified [Figure 2 image](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.23092v2/task_3.png) from [the paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.23092v2), 1740 × 828 pixels. It illustrates relationship scoring, key-frame selection, and causal explanation.

Authors: Shuxiang Zhang, Yiting Yin, Wenxuan Song, Yuhang Wu, Miao Liu. The paper lists the arXiv.org perpetual non-exclusive license; this is separate from the project repository's MIT license.

All thumbnails display the complete illustration and link to its full-resolution file.
